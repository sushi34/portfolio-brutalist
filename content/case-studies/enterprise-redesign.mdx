## The Challenge

Apex Industrial Solutions, a 40-year-old industrial equipment manufacturer, had a website that hadn't been touched since 2016. It was hurting their credibility with modern buyers and generating unqualified leads.

**Critical issues:**
- 78% bounce rate, 1:04 avg session duration
- 94% of leads unqualified (wrong industry, company size, budget)
- No clear value proposition for 3 distinct buyer personas
- 200+ pages with duplicate content, broken links, outdated specs
- Not mobile responsive (45% mobile traffic, broken experience)
- CMS impossible for marketing team to update
- Zero SEO strategy — ranking for 12 keywords total

## Research & Discovery

### Stakeholder Interviews
Interviewed 14 stakeholders across sales, marketing, engineering, and C-suite.

**Key tensions:**
- Sales: "We need more leads" vs Marketing: "We need better leads"
- Engineering: "Show all 847 product specs" vs UX: "Buyers need guided selection"
- CEO: "Look established/traditional" vs Market: "Look innovative/modern"

### User Research
- 8 customer interviews (recent buyers, lost deals, long-term clients)
- Analyzed 18 months of CRM data (HubSpot)
- Heatmaps & session recordings (Hotjar)
- SEO audit (Ahrefs, Screaming Frog)

**Buyer personas validated:**
1. **Procurement Manager** (40%): Needs specs, pricing, compliance docs fast
2. **Plant Engineer** (35%): Needs technical compatibility, integration guides
3. **Operations Director** (25%): Needs ROI case studies, total cost of ownership

### Competitive Gap Analysis
Analyzed 12 competitors. Apex ranked last in:
- Mobile usability
- Content depth
- Lead capture UX
- Technical documentation access
- Brand perception (perceived as "legacy/outdated")

## Strategy

### Three-Pillar Approach

**1. Buyer-Guided Architecture**
- 3 distinct entry points from homepage
- Dynamic product finder (guided selling)
- Persona-specific resource centers

**2. Content-First Redesign**
- Audit: 200+ pages → 68 strategic pages
- New content: 12 case studies, 24 product guides, 8 comparison pages
- SEO foundation: 200+ target keywords mapped to pages

**3. Modern Technical Foundation**
- Webflow CMS for marketing autonomy
- Component-based design system
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Core Web Vitals optimization

## Design Process

### Phase 1: IA & Wireframes (3 weeks)
- Card sorting with 12 users (open + closed)
- Tree testing validated new IA (89% success rate)
- Low-fi wireframes for 18 key templates
- Stakeholder review workshops (3 sessions)

### Phase 2: Design System (2 weeks)
- Brand refresh: refined logo, expanded color system, new typography
- 42 reusable components in Figma
- Webflow component library mirror
- Documentation in Notion

### Phase 3: High-Fidelity Design (4 weeks)
Key templates designed:
- **Homepage**: Dynamic hero, persona entry points, trust indicators
- **Product Catalog**: Faceted search, comparison tool, config wizard
- **Product Detail**: Tabbed specs, CAD downloads, compatibility checker
- **Resource Center**: Filterable library (case studies, whitepapers, videos)
- **About/Leadership**: Team, certifications, sustainability, history
- **Contact/Quote**: Multi-step form with smart routing

### Phase 4: Development & Launch (3 weeks)
- Webflow build with custom code for advanced interactions
- 301 redirect map for 180+ URLs
- Analytics migration (GA4 + enhanced ecommerce)
- Staged rollout: staging → 10% traffic → 100%

**Business impact:**
- Sales team: "First time in years we're talking to the right people"

## Technical Highlights

**Webflow + Custom Code:**
- Dynamic product catalog (CMS + custom JS for comparison)
- Multi-step quote form with conditional logic
- Client-side search (Fuse.js) for resource center
- Progressive image loading (IntersectionObserver)
- Service worker for offline resource access

**SEO Foundation:**
- Schema.org markup (Product, Organization, Breadcrumb, FAQ)
- XML sitemaps auto-generated
- hreflang ready for i18n
- Core Web Vitals monitoring via Vercel Analytics

## Reflections

**What worked:**
- Buyer-guided IA resolved internal stakeholder conflicts
- Webflow empowered marketing team (huge win for long-term)
- Content audit forced strategic decisions early
- Staged launch caught critical bugs before full rollout

**What I'd do differently:**
- Invest more in copywriting upfront (content bottleneck in Phase 3)
- Better analytics event taxonomy from day one
- Include sales team in user testing (they know the objections)
- Plan for multilingual from architecture phase
